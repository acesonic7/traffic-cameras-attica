# Data licence

The data files in this repository are made available under the
**Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

This covers `data.js`, `cameras.csv`, `tools/positions.json`, `tools/ai_positions.json`
and `tools/osm_local.json`.

## Why ODbL

Junction positions, camera positions and camera headings are derived from
OpenStreetMap data, which is © OpenStreetMap contributors and licensed under
the ODbL. A database derived from it has to be shared under the same licence.

## What you may do

- Use, copy, share and adapt the data, for any purpose, at no cost.

## What you must do

- **Attribute:** credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" and this project
  (Ioannis Tsouros, https://github.com/acesonic7).
- **Share alike:** if you publish an adapted version of the data, publish it
  under the ODbL as well.
- **Keep it open:** if you redistribute the data with technical restrictions,
  also offer a version without them.

## What the data is and is not

- The list of junctions, node numbers, traffic flows and camera counts comes
  from the Attica Traffic Police Directorate's table as published in the press
  (gocar.gr, 12 November 2024).
- The eight Artificial Intelligence camera locations come from press reports;
  no official list has been published.
- Positions marked `approximate` could not be matched to a street crossing.
- Individual camera positions and headings are estimates, not surveyed or
  official data.
